Research gave us three reasons to take the gap seriously.

gratitude

We misread the reaction.

Across three experiments, senders underestimated how positive recipients would feel and overestimated awkwardness.

Kumar & Epley, 2018 · Psychological Science
channel

Text is easy, but voice connects more.

Voice-based interactions created stronger social bonds than text, without increasing awkwardness.

Kumar & Epley, 2021 · Journal of Experimental Psychology: General
category scan

Digital cards still begin with an occasion.

Major card products organize discovery around birthdays, farewells, milestones and other occasion categories.

Hallmark eCards + Kudoboard product flows, accessed 5 Sep 2026
