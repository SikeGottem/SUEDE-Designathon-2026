What changes when the form changes?

Quick text · what research supportsText can carry genuine gratitude, but written messages remove vocal tone and can be easier to misread.The gratitude result mainly measured the sender; it does not prove equal meaning for the receiver.Sheldon & Yu, 2022 · Kruger et al., 2005
Voice · what research supportsIn controlled exchanges, hearing a voice produced more reported connection than text without increasing awkwardness.This was synchronous conversation, not a test of appreciation voice notes.Kumar & Epley, 2021
Video · what research supportsAdding video did not reliably create more connection than audio alone.The paper supports the value of voice cues, not a universal ranking of every call or message.Kumar & Epley, 2021
Handwritten letter · what research supportsPeople expressing gratitude by letter overestimated recipient awkwardness and underestimated the positive response.The study did not compare handwritten letters with text, voice or this product.Kumar & Epley, 2018
Physical gift · what research supportsPerceived effort predicted gratitude mostly when recipients interpreted that effort as thoughtfulness.Higher price did not reliably produce greater recipient appreciation.Algoe, Haidt & Gable, 2008 · Flynn & Adams, 2009
Our synthesis · still to testKeep the ease of digital communication, then make authorship, intention and receiver control easier to feel.The studies make this opportunity plausible. Only a matched-format receiver test can validate the plotted gap.Team hypothesis · not a participant finding
