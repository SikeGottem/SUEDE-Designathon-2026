Psychology evidence ledger

Three plausible mechanisms. Three explicit boundaries.

secondary evidence
01

Expression can be miscalibrated

What the studies support

Senders may underestimate positive reactions to gratitude; writers may overestimate how clearly tone travels without voice.

What it does not prove

That text is shallow, that every sender hesitates, or that our interface fixes tone.

Kumar & Epley 2018
Kruger et al. 2005
Text gratitude can still be rewarding: Sheldon & Yu 2022
02

Thoughtfulness must be legible

What the studies support

Perceived thoughtfulness can help explain recipient gratitude; price and raw effort alone do not reliably do so.

What it does not prove

That more creation time is always better, or that visible effort cannot become performance pressure.

Algoe et al. 2008
Flynn & Adams 2009
Effort heuristic is context-sensitive: Ziano et al. 2023
03

Digital ownership is designed

What the studies support

Control, identity and contextual meaning can help digital possessions feel ownable and worth keeping.

What it does not prove

Legal ownership, permanence, privacy—or that a receiver will value this specific object.

Atasoy & Morewedge 2018
Odom et al. 2012
Reminder sincerity can be fragile: Wang et al. 2024
Synthesis We translate mechanisms into testable design choices, never into guaranteed emotional outcomes. Full report WIKI/RESEARCH/FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION/PSYCHOLOGY_PITCH/report-source.md

Proposed decision rules

Finalise before recruitment and time-stamp with the instrument.

  • Continue if receivers understand the object and report more desired warmth or keeping without higher reply pressure.
  • Revise if value comes from one medium only, the making flow dominates, or ownership language remains absent.
  • Stop / pivot if ordinary text performs as well, the ritual feels more awkward, or sharing creates privacy concern.

Scope discipline

What we built now—and what evidence earns next.

bounded scope

IN THE PITCH PROTOTYPE

One complete ritual

  • name one recipient
  • author one mixed-media object
  • fold, stamp and choose a carrier
  • hand off one exact link / QR
  • receiver opens on their own terms
  • receiver keeps, closes or removes

ONLY IF THE TEST SUPPORTS IT

Next product work

  • exploratory backstage memory prompts—test first
  • secure portable media
  • recipient verification and revocation
  • account-backed retention / export
  • wrong-recipient recovery
  • tested accessible alternatives

DELIBERATELY OUT

Engagement machinery

  • feed, comments or public sharing
  • streaks, scores or relationship metrics
  • read receipts or sender surveillance
  • automatic authored messages
  • generic AI “write it for me”
  • notifications presented as validated

Four short answers

Why not text?

Text remains a valid control. We are testing whether an authored object makes thoughtfulness and keeping more legible without chat pressure.

What is innovative?

The product combines mixed-media authorship, link-level ease and a receiver-controlled ending into one ordinary-day digital-gift ritual.

What is proven?

The working flow and its technical limits. The social outcome, market gap and product superiority are not yet proven.

What would change your mind?

If matched receivers prefer ordinary text, feel more awkward, or do not value keeping the object, we revise or stop.
