## Live handling

- If time slips, compress slides 4 and 8. Never cut the unwritten rule, evidence boundary, receiver agency or return to the rule at the close.
- If asked “why not text or FaceTime?”: those remain valid controls. This prototype tests whether a separate authored object and no-reply receiving context changes anticipated awkwardness, pressure or memorability.
- If asked “why an app?”: the sender uses the making experience; the receiver opens a link without an account. The digital medium is being tested for lower logistics and mixed-media authorship, not assumed to be superior.
- If asked “who is this for?”: the current recruitment hypothesis is university students maintaining friendships across distance or changing life stages. It is not “everyone,” and it is not yet validated.
- If asked “does the cabinet mean ownership?”: no. The current prototype demonstrates that the receiver can keep, reopen or remove the object. Production retention, identity, forwarding and deletion guarantees remain unresolved.
- If asked “does the QR prove the idea?”: no. It demonstrates the receiving flow. Only completed sender/receiver research and matched-format testing can support outcome claims.

## Work still outside the presentation

- Recruit the intended audience and establish the size and contexts of the problem with transparent quantitative work.
- Interview senders and receivers separately to learn why unexpected appreciation feels welcome, awkward, obligatory, alarming or culturally inappropriate.
- Compare the same appreciation through ordinary text, voice/video, a physical or crafted control and Warm & Fuzzies.
- Observe an unaided maker-to-receiver phone walkthrough, including correction, link opening, keep/reopen and removal; record what changed afterward.
- Resolve retention, deletion, forwarding, identity, privacy, accessibility, wrong-recipient and failed-media behaviour before making production claims.
- Verify the official 2026 judging rules, deployment, QR, second-device behaviour, team details, persona provenance and any required AI/third-party disclosures.
