## Live handling

- The schedule has only seven seconds of buffer. If a transition slips, compress slides 5 and 9; never cut Chloe’s framing, the evidence boundary, receiver agency or the close.
- If asked “what is `___%`?”: it is intentionally blank. The matched-format participant test must supply it, and it stays absent if the test cannot support a clean percentage.
- If asked “why not text or FaceTime?”: those remain valid controls. This prototype tests whether an authored object and no-reply receiving context change anticipated awkwardness, pressure or memorability.
- If asked “who is this for?”: university students maintaining long-distance connections with friends and family are the team-selected first market. That is a recruitment choice, not validated demand.
- If asked about anti-doomscroll prompts: they are a future, secondary branch. No context detection or resurfacing prompt is built or tested, and reminder research suggests prompting can also weaken perceived sincerity.
- If asked “why an app?”: the sender uses the making experience; the receiver opens a link without an account. The digital medium is being tested for lower logistics and mixed-media authorship, not assumed to be superior.
- If asked “does the cabinet mean ownership?”: no. The prototype demonstrates keep, reopen and remove controls. Retention, identity, forwarding and deletion guarantees remain unresolved.
- If asked “does the QR prove the idea?”: no. It demonstrates the receiver flow. Only completed sender/receiver research and matched-format testing can support outcome claims.
