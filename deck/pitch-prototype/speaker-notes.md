## Live demo run sheet

- **3:25–3:35 — Name:** name the person; this makes an ordinary thought explicitly personal.
- **3:35–3:50 — Make:** show authorship and explain why visible effort matters without exploring every tool.
- **3:50–4:05 — Seal:** show the deliberate transition out of editing and explain why the finite ending creates closure.
- **4:05–4:25 — Send:** show the fixed receiver link and connect that choice to lower receiving friction.
- **4:25–4:40 — Open:** use the prepared receiver device or window to open that link without an account, on their own terms.
- **4:40–4:48 — Keep/remove:** show keep/reopen and the close/remove options, connecting them to receiver control without implying permanence.
- **4:48–4:55 — Finish:** land the receiver-controlled ending: **“The message can be complete when it arrives.”** Use pre-opened live tabs as the recovery path; do not add another deck slide or replace the demo with a video.

- If time slips, shorten the map narration and creation step. Do not cut What / Why / How or the receiver-controlled ending.
