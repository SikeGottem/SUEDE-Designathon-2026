## Implemented pitch-demo contract

- The pitch uses one compiled canonical artifact through two routes outside the normal product. `/demo/create` begins at the existing landing and walks ordinary maker setup before loading that prepared artifact into the normal editable creator. `/demo/receive` with no fragment opens the replayable compiled fallback; it is never consumed. Reloading either base route restores its deterministic starting state.
- The presenter can use the normal writing, material, doodle, stamp and carrier tools to customise the prepared artifact. At handoff, the app serializes the current safe edited snapshot into `/demo/receive#v3.<payload>` and generates its QR from that exact URL. A valid safe v3 fragment wins on the receiver. A recognized invalid, unsafe, or legacy payload shows the unavailable state rather than silently substituting the compiled object.
- Local blob media may be edited on the presenter device but cannot travel in the cross-device bearer fragment; it must be removed before link/QR handoff. An oversize serializable object also exposes its limit honestly. These are visible demo limits, not promises of server persistence or private delivery.
- The fixed bare `/demo/receive` QR is a stable deck fallback only. It intentionally opens the compiled canonical object, not the presenter's latest unsent edit. Before the pitch, create the desired artifact on `/demo/create`, then use its runtime-generated exact handoff QR/link for the live object; retain the bare QR and no-device fallback for recovery. Demo keep/remove state is session-only, resets on reload, and never reads or writes the normal cabinet.
- Normal draft creation, generated bearer links/QRs, receiver behaviour, and `/` and `/for/` remain unchanged. Preserve the existing paper/ink visual authenticity and add no generic demo chrome.
- The prepared-start/QR intent comes from [the 5 September pre-made artifact capture](../WIKI/TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-05-68611ccd-495c-564d-9b92-7b2c0b167533.md). The editable template and fragment handoff mechanics are Ethan's newer direct implementation decision, not transcript-derived user evidence or a private-delivery path.
- Before the pitch, rehearse the exact generated handoff link/QR on a second physical device, separately confirm the bare fallback route, and keep a no-device fallback ready.

## Current visual and interaction language

- **Before opening:** literal white field, deep ink linework, generous empty space, and one focal object. The landing and hub have no bottom navigation. The cabinet alone has a quiet `home / + / your letters` return bar so making and revisiting remain findable without turning the root into a dashboard. Cecelia's current brand board supplies steel blue `#5B7A85` plus restrained rust `#B56D5F`, ochre `#EDA343`, olive `#9F9D81`, and pale cream `#F9F9D8` material accents.
- **After opening:** full deep ink `#081F4D` with pale-cream text and marks. The white-to-deep-ink threshold is the receiving boundary, not a decorative dark mode. Small functional text stays deep ink because the lighter brand colours do not meet normal-text contrast on cream.
- **Type:** locally bundled Gaegu Regular is the working Ugly Dave-like face. Local Ugly Handwriting remains a fallback. Both stay replaceable after team review.
- **Icons and illustration:** Home, bottle, firefly, paper plane, envelope, seal base, water, reeds, and plane cloud state use Cecelia's transparent masters from `public/assets/illustrations/cecilia-collection/`. The flight firefly alternates supplied wing states, then hands over to the supplied carrying master; because that drawing already contains its payload, the app does not overlay a second envelope. Unused archive sheets remain catalogued rather than being sprayed through the UI; material controls and the small provisional sticker set remain code-native replacement slots, never emoji or a generic sticker pack.
- **Motion:** route exit/entry remains mounted long enough to render; the hub firefly enters once; camera opening, shutter/record state, capture-to-canvas transition, material landing/removal, selection, sealing, water-led bottle travel, firefly anticipation/backflutter, plane departure, reveal and cabinet placement use motion for feedback or spatial continuity. Captured video is user-controlled everywhere and never auto-plays; there are no decorative ambient loops, confetti, particles, read states, or engagement loops.
- **Viewport:** every state is locked to one phone viewport with no vertical scroll, visible scrollbar, or rubber-band movement. The keyboard reduces the live canvas height; cabinet overflow uses 2×2 pages; the opened paper keeps its authored full-screen composition while receiver actions expand only when requested.
- **No-chat contract:** there are no bubbles, reply composer, reactions, feed, notifications, streaks, public sharing, relationship scoring, or sender-visible open/keep state.

## Intentionally absent or simulated

- real login, contacts, service-grade private delivery, authentication, server upload, durable recording/storage, music licensing/playback, encryption, deletion, notification, expiry, size-limit policy, or analytics;
- real permissions, link expiry, wrong-recipient recovery, content moderation, abuse reporting, consent controls, or deployed-product accessibility guarantees;
- final name, complete brand system beyond Cecelia's current palette/type board, final material/mark/sticker masters, final visual polish, or Figma reconstruction;
- a claim that the carrier set, cabinet, no-reply language, or any opening ritual has been validated with intended users.
