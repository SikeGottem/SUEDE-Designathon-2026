## First interpretation

Treat this as a working interpretation of the user-provided wording until the official source is verified.

### What it literally asks

Create an experience that does at least one of four things to an unwritten rule: reveal it, communicate it, question it or redesign it.

### What it may actually be asking us to change

Make an invisible social rule perceptible enough that people can understand, challenge or experience a changed version of it.

### Terms that need definition

- **Experience:** a designed sequence, environment, service, object, interaction, ritual or digital product. Not automatically an app.
- **Unwritten rule:** a repeated expectation or norm that shapes behaviour without relying mainly on a formal written instruction.
- **Reveal:** make the rule and its effects visible.
- **Communicate:** help people recognise or navigate the rule.
- **Question:** create reflection, friction or a credible alternative interpretation.
- **Redesign:** change the conditions, incentives, roles or ritual that reproduce the rule.

### What most teams may assume

- Choosing a broad social norm with no specific person, setting or consequence.
- Treating “unwritten rule” as trivia to display rather than a force shaping behaviour.
- Building an app before deciding whether the experience should be spatial, social, physical, performative, service-based or digital.
- Assuming exposure automatically creates change.

### Possible overlooked angle

The most interesting response may let participants feel the rule operating, or temporarily live inside a redesigned version, instead of merely explaining it.
