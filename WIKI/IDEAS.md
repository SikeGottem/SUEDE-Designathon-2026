# Ideas

Keep directions separate until there is evidence to combine them. Each direction should be small enough to explain and specific enough to challenge.

## Two-speed idea workflow

Capture every raw thought quickly. Only plausible contenders need the complete [Idea scaffold](IDEA_SCAFFOLD.md); this protects divergence from bureaucracy while preventing a vague idea from becoming polished UI.

## Quick idea template

### Working name

One sentence: For **[specific person]** in **[specific moment]**, we could **[change]** because **[insight]**.

- Reading of the brief:
- Unwritten rule, setting and how it is enforced:
- Primary move: reveal / communicate / question / redesign
- Who benefits and who is constrained:
- Why it matters:
- Main interaction or intervention:
- Riskiest assumption:
- Fastest way to test it:
- What would make us stop:
- Evidence links:
- Current state: open / testing / paused / selected

Before an idea is recommended, compared as a finalist or converted into material UI, complete `WIKI/IDEA_SCAFFOLD.md` and run `.agents/skills/suede-idea-stress-test/SKILL.md`. The resulting packet then goes through `suede-judge-review`; the scaffold does not approve its own idea.
