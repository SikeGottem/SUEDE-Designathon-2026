# Friendship appreciation — idea review checklist

> **Use:** a compact human review list, not a decision record or evidence of user desirability. `KEEP` means it belongs in the current reversible prototype contract; `CUT` means it is not part of this product; `REVISIT` remains a real unanswered choice; `SUPERSEDED` records a later correction; `BOUNDARY` is a non-negotiable constraint.
>
> **Source shorthand:** `S1–16` are chronological early captures mapped in the transcript ingest; later references use their raw transcript ID and timestamp. The [transcript ingest](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_TRANSCRIPT_INGEST.md) and [conceptual spec](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_PRODUCT_SPEC.md) give the current synthesis; raw sources remain authoritative for what was actually said.

## 1. Core meaning and problem

1. **Ordinary-day appreciation friction — KEEP.** A person can value a friend but hesitate to say it directly because, without an accepted occasion, it may read as unusually intense, alarming, romantic, farewell-like, or demanding a matching response. This is the current working unwritten rule, not a universal population claim. **Best source:** S14 / [Recording 17](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-new-recording-17.md) 14:01–15:10, 17:02–18:25.

2. **One real person and one real thought — KEEP.** The maker begins with someone and something meaningful already in mind; the product gives that thought a form rather than assigning a relationship or manufacturing emotion. **Best source:** [Conceptual spec §6](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_PRODUCT_SPEC.md#6-who-the-first-experience-is-for).

3. **Care should be intentional, not merely efficient — KEEP.** Instant messaging is useful, but the product tests whether considered choices, composition, and a finite object can carry a thought differently from a routine chat message. It must not claim that digital is inherently better than text or a physical letter. **Best source:** S15 / [Recording 20](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-new-recording-20.md) 00:26–00:49.

4. **High-contact friendship maintenance — REVISIT.** Friends may be intact while out of touch, yet feel pressure to be constantly active and responsive. This is a useful adjacent framing, but has not been chosen as the primary rule or outcome. **Best source:** S14 / [Recording 17](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-new-recording-17.md) 19:16–20:18.

5. **Not a mental-health or male-only product — BOUNDARY.** Masculinity/toughness stigma was an early lens, but the product must not claim all men, young people, Australians, or friendships behave the same way, and must not position appreciation as mental-health treatment. **Best source:** S14 / [Recording 17](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-new-recording-17.md) 17:38–17:55; [conceptual spec §3](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_PRODUCT_SPEC.md#3-what-is-being-redesigned).

6. **Human-authored intimate content — BOUNDARY.** The maker writes, records, draws, and selects; AI may support private prompts or implementation but cannot write the appreciation in the maker's voice. **Best source:** [Conceptual spec §5.1](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_PRODUCT_SPEC.md#51-human-authored-intimacy).

7. **A finite object, not a conversation — BOUNDARY.** The experience has a beginning, opening, body, and ending; it is not chat with prettier decoration. **Best source:** [Conceptual spec §5.2](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_PRODUCT_SPEC.md#52-one-object-not-a-conversation).

8. **No artificial proof-of-care friction — BOUNDARY.** Waiting, scarcity, quotas, streaks, and busywork cannot stand in for meaningful choice. **Best source:** [Conceptual spec §5.4](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_PRODUCT_SPEC.md#54-deliberate-not-artificially-difficult).

## 2. Creation materials and interactions

9. **Digital letter by private link — KEEP, evolved.** The early core was a custom written letter opened through a link; it remains the written centre but has evolved beyond a text-only letter. **Best source:** S14 / [Recording 17](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-new-recording-17.md) 13:48–14:10.

10. **Letter plus scrapbook / care-package object — KEEP.** The strongest form direction is one sendable, spatially composed object that combines a written core with selected memory materials rather than a card template or form wizard. **Best source:** S14 / [Recording 17](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-new-recording-17.md) 18:32–18:46; S16 / [Recording 19](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-new-recording-19.md) 00:48–02:27.

11. **Blank physical paper first — KEEP.** The Studio opens directly on a white paper surface with restrained hand-drawn lines, not a camera permission request, media background, or a faux-paper texture. **Best source:** [a9499b3d…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-a9499b3d-07e2-5182-bdfd-a9a85f9860db.md) 03:55–04:48.

12. **Story-like ease, not Instagram imitation — KEEP.** The maker needs mobile-native speed, sparse tools, and direct manipulation, but no Instagram branding, feed, posting, expiry, views, reactions, or social mechanics. **Best source:** [d4b7ec93…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-d4b7ec93-efb6-516d-99da-c84cfa746154.md) 21:24–22:18.

13. **Full-screen, one-page creator — KEEP.** Making should occupy attention as one evolving composition, not separate recipient/reason/media form screens or a dense Canva-style editor. **Best source:** [Conceptual spec §10](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_PRODUCT_SPEC.md#10-page-1--studio).

14. **Recipient as lightweight anchor — KEEP.** The intended person remains editable within the workspace rather than becoming an administrative step, friend list, or progress form. **Best source:** [Conceptual spec §10](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_PRODUCT_SPEC.md#10-page-1--studio).

15. **Words are central and directly editable — KEEP.** Writing should be the maker's own core; it can be placed, edited, moved, rotated, and removed in the composition. **Best source:** [d4b7ec93…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-d4b7ec93-efb6-516d-99da-c84cfa746154.md) 22:18.

16. **Optional rich-media palette — KEEP, scope open.** Photos, short video, voice, song/sound, drawing, restrained maker-selected colour, stickers, marks, and arrangement may carry specific memory, voice, authorship, or state change; no object needs every material. Exact palette and sticker set remain open. **Best source:** [Transcript ingest — The object](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_TRANSCRIPT_INGEST.md#the-object); [bbc5485c…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-bbc5485c-837c-538c-b9e0-855e7aec8060.md) 05:28.

16a. **Selectable page-design/page-character variation — KEEP as a small approved set.** The creator needs more personalisation than the current one-look scrapbook treatment. Offer a small selectable page-character set that still leaves writing, materials, and arrangement to the maker. It is not a public template marketplace or multi-page product flow; exact taxonomy and count must remain deliberately small. **Best source:** [0c2a02aa…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-0c2a02aa-848e-5dae-9fe0-339d566da09a.md) 00:00; Ethan's direct 5 September approval.

16b. **Handwriting and strike-through editing — KEEP for the prototype.** Typed words appear in the working handwritten treatment. Backspace marks words with a hand-drawn strike-through rather than deleting them, preserving the letter's physical-writing feeling; an unobtrusive keyboard-accessible undo/restore route is mandatory. **Best source:** Ethan's direct 5 September approval.

16c. **Fold, envelope decoration and custom seal — KEEP.** Finishing the letter creates a new maker stage: fold the composed paper, select one of a small set of envelope structures, decorate it, draw/apply a personal seal, then choose the carrier. The envelope is a meaningful authored outer layer, not a cosmetic send confirmation or package market. **Best source:** Ethan's direct 5 September approval.

17. **Camera/media as optional placed material — KEEP.** Camera and existing media are secondary actions; a captured image/video lands on paper as a small movable Polaroid-like item, not as the whole canvas. **Best source:** [a9499b3d…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-a9499b3d-07e2-5182-bdfd-a9a85f9860db.md) 03:55–04:48.

18. **Real freehand doodling — KEEP.** Drawing must be a usable doodle surface, not a random decorative mark or a fixed sticker. **Best source:** [a9499b3d…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-a9499b3d-07e2-5182-bdfd-a9a85f9860db.md) 07:17.

19. **Direct manipulation of selected pieces — KEEP.** Text and materials are selected in place, moved, rotated, edited/removed, and allowed to overlap where readable; controls vanish when not needed. **Best source:** [Conceptual spec §10](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_PRODUCT_SPEC.md#10-page-1--studio).

20. **Optional reflective cues — KEEP.** Prompts such as favourite memory, what they taught me, one word, or a small noticed moment may help only when requested; they never become mandatory fields or AI-written content. **Best source:** [Transcript ingest — Creator experience](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_TRANSCRIPT_INGEST.md#the-creator-experience).

21. **Creator controls need progressive disclosure — KEEP.** A compact thumb-reachable rail/tray is acceptable, but no permanent prompt chips, full-time media controls, equally weighted cards, tutorial overload, or infinite feature wall. **Best source:** [65e72fa1…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-65e72fa1-b8b1-5b23-869d-dd63f1a92d6b.md) 32:28–33:40; [conceptual spec §10](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_PRODUCT_SPEC.md#10-page-1--studio).

22. **Shared journal/scrapbook — SUPERSEDED.** A reciprocal collaborative memory space was explored, but the current object is intentionally one-way and finite to avoid a chat/reply loop. **Best source:** S14 / [Recording 17](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-new-recording-17.md) 11:55–13:09.

23. **Random friend / daily gratitude prompt / streak — CUT.** The BeReal-like idea of assigning a friend each day was explicitly early exploration; voluntary, occasion-free authorship replaced it. **Best source:** S14 / [Recording 17](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-new-recording-17.md) 14:42–15:27; [transcript ingest — Superseded](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_TRANSCRIPT_INGEST.md#superseded-rejected-or-historical-only).

24. **Physical e-ink letter with delivery delay — CUT.** A tactile device with letters taking days to arrive was a useful reference but conflicts with digital-only delivery and the boundary against forced delay as proof of sincerity. **Best source:** S14 / [Recording 17](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-new-recording-17.md) 14:16–14:37.

## 3. Delivery and opening ritual

25. **Private account-light link — KEEP as a real coded prototype path.** The receiver should not need to install the product; the maker gives one private link outside the experience. The prototype/demo must use a real receiver-facing link and QR, while link privacy, expiry, storage, size limits and wrong-recipient recovery remain honestly scoped. **Best source:** S16 / [Recording 19](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-new-recording-19.md) 02:27–02:53; Ethan's direct 5 September approval.

26. **Standalone object, not WhatsApp/Instagram integration — KEEP.** Platform integration was considered, but an independent link gives the recipient a distinct private threshold instead of another familiar conversation surface. **Best source:** S16 / [Recording 19](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-new-recording-19.md) 02:27–02:53.

27. **Preview shows sealed exterior only — KEEP for the current carrier test.** Before giving, the preview should show the selected vessel (explicitly: bottle) alone, not interior words/media spilling below it; full content belongs after opening. **Best source:** [913ddc06…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-913ddc06-3a86-513e-a05c-3c0bbe1d4312.md) 07:58.

28. **Small carrier set — KEEP as a reversible test, after envelope authorship.** Bottle, firefly, and paper plane are the current hand-drawn send metaphors; the maker chooses only after making, folding, decorating and sealing the letter. This is not a package marketplace, multiple-theme selector, or entry task. **Best source:** [166f8cfb…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-166f8cfb-39ee-57ce-9eaa-8b1a114657af.md) 00:18, 04:59; [4ef45d20…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-4ef45d20-2643-5baa-97e2-ea66e5810701.md) 03:26–05:24; Ethan's direct 5 September approval.

29. **Envelope before carrier — KEEP.** The letter folds, the maker decorates an envelope and applies a custom seal, then chooses bottle, firefly, or paper plane. The envelope stage is part of authorship and has equal weight to carrier choice; it does not assert that any carrier has a universal meaning. **Best source:** Ethan's direct 5 September approval.

30. **One-time courier arrival/departure — KEEP for the current carrier test.** On give, a courier collects the sealed object and flies away; on arrival, it flies in holding the object and drops it centre before opening. Its motion must be slow enough to read as transport, not ambient decoration. **Best source:** [913ddc06…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-913ddc06-3a86-513e-a05c-3c0bbe1d4312.md) 05:06, 07:58; [2d8ab5ab…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-2d8ab5ab-b58f-5864-98e7-b63865cb6c37.md) 15:29.

31. **Firefly is not a persistent mascot — BOUNDARY.** The firefly replaces the ladybug only as the one-shot carrier/courier role in the handoff/arrival event, never as a product pet, navigation helper, streak loop, or generic AI mascot. **Best source:** [166f8cfb…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-166f8cfb-39ee-57ce-9eaa-8b1a114657af.md) 00:18, 04:59; [DECISIONS — carrier review](DECISIONS.md#2026-09-05---test-a-bounded-carrier-and-opening-delta).

32. **Double-tap opening, with direct fallback and direct full-object unfold — REVISIT.** The newest delivery feedback says double-tap the delivered object; earlier bottle testing used a cork swipe/drag. Test double-tap as the default choreography, retain an explicit keyboard/touch/reduced-motion Open route, and let the fold reveal the full composed object directly rather than a bare state swap or intermediate transition. Carrier-specific opening rituals and detailed receiver-led media reveal are parked. **Best source:** [913ddc06…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-913ddc06-3a86-513e-a05c-3c0bbe1d4312.md) 05:06; [bbc5485c…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-bbc5485c-837c-538c-b9e0-855e7aec8060.md) 05:13–05:28; Ethan's direct 5 September approval.

33. **White-to-navy opening threshold — KEEP.** The maker/unopened state remains white; opening moves the receiver into a private navy world. The colour transition must do the work of a threshold, not merely decorate. **Best source:** [Conceptual spec §8](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_PRODUCT_SPEC.md#8-the-overall-emotional-arc).

34. **No anonymous delivery — BOUNDARY.** The receiver knows who made the object before intimate content appears, and has a leave/return/close route. **Best source:** [Conceptual spec §13](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_PRODUCT_SPEC.md#13-page-4--arrival).

35. **No forced delivery delay — BOUNDARY.** Delay cannot be used to manufacture sincerity, even if some physical-letter references have that quality. **Best source:** [Conceptual spec §5.4](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_PRODUCT_SPEC.md#54-deliberate-not-artificially-difficult).

36. **Animation-ready separated assets — KEEP.** The firefly needs at least two wing states, and the carried letter/object must be a separate asset/layer so it can be picked up and dropped. Exact second wing pose remains open. **Best source:** [913ddc06…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-913ddc06-3a86-513e-a05c-3c0bbe1d4312.md) 04:21–04:44; [166f8cfb…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-166f8cfb-39ee-57ce-9eaa-8b1a114657af.md) 00:18, 04:59.

## 4. Receiver ownership and archive

37. **Receiver-controlled attention and ending — KEEP.** The receiver can open now, leave, return later, close, keep, or remove without sending a relational signal to the maker. **Best source:** [Conceptual spec §§13–17](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_PRODUCT_SPEC.md#13-page-4--arrival).

38. **No reply, reactions, receipts, or sender telemetry — BOUNDARY.** Giving ends for the maker after handoff: no opened/kept/replayed status, no nudge, no response composer, no engagement score. **Best source:** [Conceptual spec §5.5](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_PRODUCT_SPEC.md#55-receiver-first-after-handoff).

39. **Receiver-led media reveal — PARKED.** Words remain central and nothing may auto-play, but the detailed receiver-controlled media-reveal interaction is deferred from the current approved build scope. **Best source:** Ethan's direct 5 September approval.

40. **Private cabinet / memory collection — KEEP as a persistent multi-object prototype branch.** A kept object becomes a distinct, replayable thing in a private cabinet closer to a memory box, record shelf, or camera roll than an inbox/feed. Service-level permanence, export, revocation and storage semantics remain open. **Best source:** [Conceptual spec §16](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_PRODUCT_SPEC.md#16-page-7--receiver-ending-and-private-cabinet); Ethan's direct 5 September approval.

41. **Cabinet service-level permanence, export, revocation, and deletion model — REVISIT.** The prototype retains multiple distinct objects, but storage duration, removal semantics, export and any sender control for a real service are not settled. **Best source:** [Transcript ingest — Still open](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_TRANSCRIPT_INGEST.md#still-open); Ethan's direct 5 September approval.

42. **3D tree archive — CUT.** A rotating 3D tree archive was explored, then rejected as low-leverage scope for the sprint; retain the simpler cabinet. **Best source:** [65e72fa1…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-65e72fa1-b8b1-5b23-869d-dd63f1a92d6b.md) 07:48–10:08.

## 5. Visual and motion direction

43. **Handmade, team-authored visual language — KEEP.** Use Gaegu/working ugly-handwritten type, airy space, imperfect linework, and real team-drawn recurring assets; do not let generated stand-ins become final art direction. **Best source:** [Transcript ingest — Reference boundary](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_TRANSCRIPT_INGEST.md#reference-boundary).

44. **Reference grammar, not `grug` identity — BOUNDARY.** Borrow the supplied reference's restraint, hand-drawn marks, and colour-field logic, never its name, wordmark, flower/sunrise copy, mascot, or exact screen. **Best source:** [Transcript ingest — Reference boundary](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_TRANSCRIPT_INGEST.md#reference-boundary).

45. **Avoid AI-looking surface and decorative clutter — BOUNDARY.** Do not use generic mascot identity, gradients/particles, fake sketch filters, random CSS imperfection, decorative backdrop paths, visible motion/path guides, equal cards, or a generic scrapbook collage to simulate authorship. **Best source:** [Conceptual spec §18](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_PRODUCT_SPEC.md#18-motion-and-sound-language); [2d8ab5ab…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-2d8ab5ab-b58f-5864-98e7-b63865cb6c37.md) 15:29.

46. **Purposeful motion across the whole experience — KEEP.** Motion should clarify selection, placement, courier departure/arrival, opening, media state, and cabinet acceptance; use an equivalent reduced-motion route and never rely on ambient animation. **Best source:** [d4b7ec93…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-d4b7ec93-efb6-516d-99da-c84cfa746154.md) 17:04; [conceptual spec §18](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_PRODUCT_SPEC.md#18-visual-system-and-craft-boundary).

47. **White → navy emotional arc — KEEP.** White is making/unopened possibility; navy is receiver privacy, attention, and memory. Do not use navy as glossy sender branding. **Best source:** [Conceptual spec §8](FRIENDSHIP_APPRECIATION_PRODUCT_SPEC.md#8-the-overall-emotional-arc).

48. **Home/carrier simplification — KEEP.** Home stays minimal and bottom-centred; carrier selection shows one object in negative space with purposeful transition motion, not route waves, helper copy, or a mascot explanation. **Best source:** [4bf6522b…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-4bf6522b-ed6e-5d7d-a2d6-62ec7981fd4e.md) 01:26–03:17.

49. **Firefly as current one-shot carrier/courier — KEEP for the reversible test.** Replace the ladybug with Cecelia's supplied firefly in the limited carrier trio and use it only for handoff/arrival. It cannot become a swarm, persistent pet, mascot, navigation device, or streak system. Preserve separate wing-state and carried-object layers for motion. **Best source:** [166f8cfb…](TRANSCRIPTS/2026-09-04-166f8cfb-39ee-57ce-9eaa-8b1a114657af.md) 00:18, 04:59; Ethan's direct 5 September asset handoff.
